🐉 Creatures Encyclopedia

🐉 Dragon (Dragon / ड्रैगन / Дракон / تنين)

Kya hai: Sabse famous legendary creature — serpent/reptile jaisa, wings, aag chhodne wala (Western) ya paani/mausam ka control (Eastern/Chinese).
Dikh kaise: Lamba sharir, scales, seengh, bade wings (Western), lambi sarp jaisi body (Eastern).
Real ya fake? Science me proof nahi — ye mythology/legends me hai. Har culture me dragon stories hain, isliye manavyan isse "real feeling" me dekhte hain. Culturally bohot real, physically unproven.

🧿 Yeti / Bigfoot

Kya hai: Himalaya (Yeti) aur North America (Bigfoot) ke bade, baalon wale humanoid.
Dikh: 7–10 feet, brown/white fur, bade pair ke nishaan.
Real? Kabhi koi verified body/proof nahi mila. Footprints mostly hoaxes nikle. Legend category.

🧜 Mermaid (मत्स्यकन्या / Sirena)

Kya hai: Aadhi insaan, aadhi machhli — samundar ki stories har continent me hain.
Real? Sailors ne dugongs/manatees ko galti se mermaid samjha — scientifically ye explanation accepted hai.

👻 Ghosts & Spirits

Kya hai: Har culture me ruh/atma ki stories. Real? Experiences real feel hote hain lakhs of logon ko, but laboratory proof ab tak nahi. Is site pe hum inki stories aur experiences study karte hain, claim nahi karte.
