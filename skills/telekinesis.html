🌀 Telekinesis — Step by Step Guide

Step 1: Focus & Calm

Din me 10 min meditation karo. Dimag shant ho to energy focus hoti hai. Breathing exercise →

Step 2: Psi Wheel (sabse pehla practice)

1. Ek paper ko square kaato, usko pencil ki tip pe balance karo (pyramid shape).
2. Haath dono side rakho, bina touch kiye.
3. Socho ki energy haath se wheel me ja rahi hai — "ghumo ghumo" imagine karo.
4. Roz 15 min. Pehla movement dikhne me hafton lag sakte hain — patience! 🙏

Step 3: Psi Ball → Move Object

Pehle psi ball banao (haatho ke beech energy ball feel karo — Chi Ball Trainer →), phir usi energy ko object pe direct karo. Lightweight object se start: paper, ping-pong ball.

Step 4: Daily Routine

  • Morning: 5 min meditation
  • Afternoon: 15 min psi wheel
  • Night: journal me likho aaj kya feel hua
