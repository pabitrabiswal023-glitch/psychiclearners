🧠 Telepathy — Step by Step

Step 1: Sender & Receiver Game

Dost ke saath karo. Ek card (red/blue) pakdo, uske rang ko strongly socho. Dost guess kare. 20 tries karo, score likho — chance se zyada correct = progress! 📈

Step 2: Number Sending

1–10 me ek number socho, partner ko bhejo: uska chehra imagine karo, number ki energy bhejo. Results journal me likho.

Step 3: Dream Telepathy

Sone se pehle kisi ko socho, usko message "bhejo". Subah usse poocho — kabhi kabhi match hota hai!
