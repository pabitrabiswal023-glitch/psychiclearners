🔮 Intuition — Step by Step

Step 1: Coin Test

Ek coin toss karo, guess karo heads/tails. 50 tries. Baseline ~50% — 60%+ consistently = strong intuition building.

Step 2: First Feeling Trust

Roz chhoti decisions (kaunsa rasta, kaunsa song) — pehla feeling follow karo aur note karo ki sahi tha ya nahi.

Step 3: Zener Cards

5 symbols wale cards banao, partner ke saath guess game karo. Ya online Zener card tests search karo.
